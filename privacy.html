Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Hermes is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner. It has no other users.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, Hermes accesses the owner's own Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts) through Google's APIs.

How the data is used

The data is used only to give the owner summaries, reminders and notes. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models outside of providing this service to the owner.

Storage

Access tokens and any notes produced are kept on a private server controlled by the owner. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
